List of terms

Abbreviations and terms that may come up in lessons:

  • inf. = informal
  • f. = formal
  • hon. = honorific

    • Honorific language shows respect to the subject of the sentence regardless of the audience. If you are talking to a friend about a teacher, you would use informal language with honorifics.

  • ant. = antonym
  • syn. = synonym
  • A = adjective
  • V = verb
  • N = noun
  • An adjective or verb stem is the word without the 다 ending.
