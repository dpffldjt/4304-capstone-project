우리 같이 웃었던 날, 영화를 틀어놓고

  • A/V~았/었던 recollection
    • ~았/었던 lets the speaker recall an experience they had in the past, related to the current time.
  • V~아/어 놓다
    • ~아/어놓다 attaches to verbs that were done and left in that state.
    • ex. 문을 열어요. Open the door.
      문을 열어놓아요. Open the door (and leave it open).

이유 없지 사랑에 대하여

  • N~에 대해/대하여 about N
    • The more common form of this grammar is 대해, with 대하여 being more poetic or old-fashioned.
    • ~에 대해 attaches to nouns to give the meaning of something being "about" the noun.
    • It can be used as an adjective phrase using 대한.
    • ex. 사랑에 대한 이유 없다. There is no reason about love.
